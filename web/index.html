BREACH CONTEXT

In early 2025, a massive leak containing data from Indian mobile operators began circulating online. HiTeckGroop was claimed as the source, but this information is unverified.

The data contains 1.8 billion records, although the number of unique users is lower at about 300 million. Each record includes a full name, father's name, and document number. This is most often an Aadhaar number, although passports and taxpayer numbers also appear.

Each row contains up to two phone numbers, but because one user may have several records, the total number of known phone numbers for a person can sometimes reach several dozen. Some records also contain usernames and email addresses.
